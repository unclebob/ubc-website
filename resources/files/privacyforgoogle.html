Home Monitor Nest – Privacy Policy

Home Monitor Nest is a private tool used only by Robert C. Martin to monitor the Google Nest thermostats in his own homes. With his authorization, it reads thermostat status (temperature, humidity, setpoint, mode, and connectivity) through Google's Smart Device Management API.

This data is used only to alert Robert to problems and to include in his own daily home report. It is not sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

Robert can revoke access at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.
